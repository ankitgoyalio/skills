# Assets and evidence

Compose with supplied brand and product assets first. Set the crop using the information the image must retain, and test that crop at the narrow layout before repeating it across a gallery.

A product preview should use its actual interface or a working component. Label an illustrative mockup in the rendered page. Generated imagery can establish mood or illustrate a concept; it cannot verify product appearance or capability.

When an asset is missing, choose a typographic composition or a visibly labeled placeholder that names the required replacement. A placeholder must not look like an existing customer or shipped product.

Customer logos, quotes, metrics, availability, and specifications require supplied or traceable evidence. Label prototype samples visibly. Keep testimonial excerpts accurate and retain attribution. Record image and font licensing requirements alongside their sources.
