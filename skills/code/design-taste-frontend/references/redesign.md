# Redesign

Inspect the affected pages or supplied artifacts before choosing a treatment. For implementation, capture narrow and wide baseline views and identify the routes, content, and integration contracts the proposed changes touch. For an artifact review, record which of those the artifact cannot reveal.

Match the intervention to the requested scope. A visual refresh can change typography, spacing, or asset presentation within working sections. Recompose sections whose structure obstructs the intended reading order. A new identity requires a brief that authorizes brand change.

Keep URLs, content meaning, legal copy, brand identity, and integration contracts unless the brief authorizes changes. Existing purple palettes and serif typography are evidence of identity, not defects. If a necessary change exceeds the brief, prepare its concrete replacement or mapping for review.

For implementation, compare affected journeys with the baseline and account for every changed contract. For authorized route migrations, verify redirects and metadata. For a design or artifact review, describe the acceptance checks that need a running site.
